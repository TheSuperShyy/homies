What to fix first

We are missing, in the order it hurts residents

  1. Elevator faults. Needs the per-building elevator company list from Yariv.
  2. The 23-hour nudge. Cheap: a cron over open tickets, no template needed.
  3. Photo to the team. Covered if the team works in the dashboard.
  4. Quote leads board. A second place to write; the owner decides.

Do not copy from the old bot

  • Silent close on any unanswered question.
  • Chats assigned to "Unknown user", so house-manager and quote chats may have reached nobody.
  • The office email as the only record of an elevator fault.

Open for the owner: whether a bot ticket should also land on the old Monday board before cutover; whether the elevator branch asks the resident or looks the company up per building; whether to build the 23-hour nudge at all. Still unknown: the wording of the old "done" message, the 23-hour trigger, and the flow created 30 Jul 2026 (Untitled).
