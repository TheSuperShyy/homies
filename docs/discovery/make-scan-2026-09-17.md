## The short version

**The WhatsApp chat scripts are not here.** Every WhatsApp scenario starts
with a webhook that ManyChat calls *after* a conversation has finished, and
Make's only job is to file the answers. The questions, their order, the
buttons and the Hebrew wording live in ManyChat, under the connection Make
lists as `nir` (id 1295408). Meta Business Manager holds the number and the
message templates, not ManyChat's flows. To extract the scripts, someone needs
ManyChat access: an invite to the ManyChat account, or a share link / export
of each flow from Nir. See "What to ask Nir for" at the end.

What Make *does* give us is the exact list of fields each flow collects, which
is the question list in disguise, and which Monday boards everything lands on.
