THIS IS THE SOURCE, NOT THE DEPLOYMENT.
The bot does not read this file. These facts are written into the system

project has already lost an afternoon to exactly that — the greeting lived in
the prompt and in the menu node, one was changed, and residents kept getting
the old one.

  a specific ticket — "yours will be done tomorrow" remains forbidden — but it
  is still Homies' promise being repeated at scale. Confirm they are happy for
  it to be quoted.


RULES ATTACHED TO THESE FACTS IN THE PROMPT
-------------------------------------------

Recorded here so they are not lost if the prompt is ever rewritten:

* Phone, address and email are QUOTED, never rephrased or reformatted. A
  resident copies them; a wrong number is worse than no number.

* Service levels are policy, not a promise about anyone's ticket. A specific
  ticket is answered only from get_request_status.

* Answer the question, do not recite the list. Item 6 has thirteen entries;
  somebody asking whether cleaning is included gets "yes, cleaning is
  included".

* Never adjudicate responsibility. Item 12 ends with "in case of doubt,
  contact us", and that ending is the operative part: where it is not
  perfectly clear, the bot says we will check and hands over. Getting it wrong
  costs a resident money.

* Anything not on this page does not exist. No staff names, no prices, no
  contract clauses beyond these. Those go to the team rather than being
  guessed at.
