# Incoming calls, 4 Oct: "היי" first, and "how are you" once (7 Claude-played calls)

The opener and prompt after the 4 Oct change (`docs/assistant/demo-inbound.md`, ready, not live), one
Claude player per call standing in for gpt-4.1, the tools answering from fixtures. No model call, nothing
spent. The context was built from the repo and asserted equal to the prompt `vapi_sync.py` sends. Every
caller is invented; the only building is בר כוכבא 23.

**Result.** A caller who only greets ("היי", "הלו?") gets a greeting back and how he is, nothing else, and
after his answer, how Michael can help. A caller who says how he is and asks back (`owner_replay`, the
owner's own words of 4 Oct, and `fine_and_you_he`) gets a warm answer and how Michael can help, with no
second how-are-you. A caller who only asks how Michael is gets an answer and the question back. A caller
who starts with the matter is not asked how he is. The feminine switch lands on "אני צריכה" and "אני
גרה". Every call ends on ולהתראות.
