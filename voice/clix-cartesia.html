Debt line — אה three times

אה, רציתי לעדכן אותך לגבי, אה, החוב שלך, שהוא מאה שקלים. במערכת שלנו הוא עדיין לא הוסדר, ו, אה, צריך להסדיר אותו עד סוף השבוע.

This is the comparison that matters. Cartesia gives the hesitation +3.84s over its own clean version; Azure gives it +1.46s. Cartesia treats אה as a real hesitation. Azure treats it as a syllable to get through.

Expression is not free. Longer speech means longer calls, and calls are billed by the minute — a 35% slower voice pushes the 5,000 min/month estimate toward 6,750. Cartesia exposes generationConfig.speed (0.6–1.5) and experimentalControls.speed, so this is tunable rather than fixed. Worth deciding how much of the extra time is the expression you wanted and how much is just slow.
