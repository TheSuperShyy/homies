Echo Stone — pick the ten seconds

Cartesia clones from a clip of up to 10 seconds. The clone is only ever as good as the ten seconds it was built from — so these are cut to begin and end inside a natural pause, with no long pause in between.

The first attempt optimised for no silence at all, which sounded chopped for a reason: a window containing no pauses necessarily starts and ends mid-word. These land on breaths instead.

What to listen for. Not audio quality — all three measure fine. Listen for whether it sounds like you talking, because that is what gets copied:

  • No pauses. Cartesia's docs say pauses in the clip are mimicked by the clone. A gap here becomes a voice that hesitates on every call.
  • Ordinary pace. If you were reading, or speeding up, the clone inherits it and there is no setting that undoes it.
  • Nothing clipped at the ends — no half-word at the start or finish.

Then clone from whichever you picked:

python scripts/voice_clone.py --go for A, or python scripts/voice_clone.py --go --clip voice/clone-candidate-b.wav
