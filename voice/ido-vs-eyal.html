Ido's clone, five ways

You asked why a three-minute recording was being used ten seconds at a time. It should not have been. Two things I had written down as facts were wrong, and this page is the experiment they were hiding.

Ten seconds is Cartesia's minimum, not its maximum. Their guide says a clone can be made "with as little as 10 seconds of audio" — a floor, which this repo had recorded as a ceiling since 5 August. They accept up to 60 seconds. Every clip cut so far threw away 210 seconds of a 220-second recording.

And the current model was never tried. sonic-3.6 speaks Hebrew and appeared in no file in this repo; the probe that concluded "only sonic-3 and sonic-preview do Hebrew" simply never guessed its name.

The two mistakes covered for each other. Cartesia: "Only Sonic 3.6 uses reference audio beyond 10 seconds, so a longer clip won't improve results on older models." A longer clip on sonic-3 would have changed nothing audible — so testing either fix alone would have looked like a dead end. That is why row four is on this page: it is the control, and it is expected to sound no better than row two.

The 55-second clip runs from 29.95s to 85.42s of the recording, cut from one pause edge to the next so no word is clipped at either end — the fault that produced v1. It peaks at −0.9 dB, so no clipping is baked in. The v2 clip was 9.4 seconds, below Cartesia's own 10-second floor; the script now refuses anything shorter.

What I got wrong, in order. Three theories about the intonation, all refuted: not short lines, not the joined clip, and not the recording being expressive — that last was my own pitch tracker making octave errors, with 99% of the "high pitch" turning out to be one-to-nineteen-frame flicker. Then the two constants above, which no amount of measuring would have caught because I had written them myself and never re-read the source. The numbers on this page are duration only. Duration can show a line is rushed. It cannot hear a clipped word or an unsettling contour, and every fault in this voice so far was found by you in seconds and by my measurements never.

Nothing is wired to any agent. Both Hebrew assistants still use Eyal. The clones are private voices on the client's Cartesia account. Regenerate this audio with python scripts/ido_compare.py.

If none of the five sound right, the honest read is that instant cloning is not going to get there and the input needs to change: Cartesia's professional cloning wants thirty minutes of audio and we have three minutes forty. That means asking Ido to record again, or keeping Eyal — who is live, working, and was chosen out of eleven at an audition.
